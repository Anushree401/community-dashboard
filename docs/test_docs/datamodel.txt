users: User profiles and account metadata

communities: Community details and visibility

memberships: Membership status and community-specific roles

posts: Community discussions

comments: Replies to posts

events: Event information

eventRsvps: Event participation

announcements: Community announcements

engagementActivities: Auditable interaction records

pointLedger: Idempotent engagement-point awards

badges: Badge definitions and achievements

Document IDs, composite indexes, transaction boundaries, pagination, and Firestore Rules will be decided based on the actual queries and permissions. We'll avoid storing an entire community's members or posts inside one document.
